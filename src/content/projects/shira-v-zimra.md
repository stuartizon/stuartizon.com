Shira v'Zimra (שירה וזמרה — "Song and Music") is a project dedicated to the preservation and dissemination of the Western Ashkenazi synagogue music tradition.

The project encompasses original recordings of liturgical music, arrangements, and educational content aimed at both practitioners and enthusiasts of Jewish sacred music.
